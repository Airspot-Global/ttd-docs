Campaign Pacing Stability Improvements[](#pacing-stability-upgrade)

##### February 6, 2026

| What you need to know and do |
| **Change Summary** | 

We improved the pacing system to increase stability and reduce instances of overpacing during sudden shifts in market dynamics. This improvement helps the platform respond more effectively to large changes in available inventory, resulting in more consistent delivery and spend behavior.

 |
| **Action Required** | 

No action is required.
