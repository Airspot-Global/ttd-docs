# External maintenance schedule

To ensure uptime and stability of the platform, The Trade Desk conducts regular external maintenance.

| Maintenance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cadence | Monthly |
| Day | Every third Friday |
| Start Time | 7:00 PM PDT |
| End Time | 8:00 PM PDT |

During the maintenance window, the platform UI, API, and external sandbox will be inaccessible but will become available immediately after the maintenance is completed. Please plan accordingly.

If there are any changes in the maintenance schedule, we will be sure to notify you in a timely manner.

We appreciate your patience and understanding. If you have questions or concerns, please contact your Account Manager or Technical Account Manager.
